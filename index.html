Orbit Techsol India, Pune

Every client wanted something different, so the work was mostly translation: sit with their people across regional offices, understand what they actually needed to segment and why, then turn that into rule sets, access control matrices and a topology that matched their compliance position.

Labs

Built to break, then watched

Enterprise firewall policy

Palo Alto PAN-OS, EVE-NG

A virtual enterprise network with DMZ, internal LAN and guest zones, with a PAN-OS next-generation firewall between them. Forty-odd rules using App-ID, User-ID, URL filtering and threat prevention profiles, and the implicit any-any allows taken out.

Then I attacked it from a Kali host with port scans, exploit attempts and malicious DNS lookups, and read the firewall logs to check the policy hits and IPS triggers against what each rule was meant to do.

I also wrote a reference mapping PAN-OS policy settings to their FortiGate equivalents, for cross-platform migration work.

Attack and defence

Deakin SIT716

A dual-router screened subnet with a full Cisco-style ACL rule set, and IP-in-IP, GRE and SSH tunnels between Kali and Ubuntu hosts.

On Mininet topologies I ran IP spoofing, ping floods, reflection and TCP SYN floods with hping3 and Metasploit, measured the CPU and memory cost of each attack, and confirmed the traffic in Wireshark. Also demonstrated DHCP starvation against a lab server, and used Snort logs to pick out command and control traffic from Betabot, Zeus and Pony Downloader.

About

I came to security through networks rather than the other way around. My internship in Pune had me deploying firewalls at client sites, and most of the work there was not the configuration itself. It was working out what each client actually needed to keep apart, and from whom.

Since then I have finished a Master of Cyber Security at Deakin and spent the labs building things specifically so I could attack them and watch what the logs said. I am more interested in the gap between what a policy is supposed to do and what it does than in collecting tools.
